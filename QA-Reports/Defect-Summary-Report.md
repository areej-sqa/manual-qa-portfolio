## Defect Status Definitions

- **Open** — Defect reported and awaiting action.
- **In Progress** — Development work is in progress.
- **Ready for Retest** — Fix is available for QA verification.
- **Reopened** — Defect still occurs after verification.
- **Closed** — Fix has been successfully verified.
- **Deferred** — Fix postponed to a future release.

## Severity Definitions

### Critical
Application failure, major security/privacy issue, data loss, duplicate financial transaction, or complete failure of a critical business workflow.

### High
Major functionality is broken or significantly affected with no reasonable workaround.

### Medium
Functionality is affected but a workaround exists or the issue does not block the main workflow.

### Low
Minor functional, UI, usability, or cosmetic issue with limited impact.
